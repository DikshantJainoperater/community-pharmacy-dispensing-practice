# 🏥 Daily Dispensing Workflow

A typical working day follows a structured process to ensure every prescription is supplied safely and accurately.

```text
Patient Arrives
        │
        ▼
Prescription Received
(NHS / Private / Veterinary)
        │
        ▼
Prescription Download
(Electronic Prescription Service)
        │
        ▼
Medicine Selection
        │
        ▼
Labelling
        │
        ▼
Assembly of Prescription
        │
        ▼
Expiry Date Verification
        │
        ▼
Preparation for Pharmacist Accuracy Check
        │
        ▼
Medicine Approved by Pharmacist
        │
        ▼
Patient Collection
        │
        ▼
Patient Safety Maintained
```

# ⚙️ Dispensing Responsibilities

My daily responsibilities include:

- Receiving prescriptions
- Downloading electronic NHS prescriptions
- Selecting medicines
- Printing and applying dispensing labels
- Counting tablets
- Measuring liquid medicines
- Preparing inhalers
- Handling refrigerated medicines
- Organising prescriptions for pharmacist accuracy checking
- Supplying completed prescriptions after pharmacist approval
- Maintaining organised dispensary workflow

Every prescription is prepared according to Boots Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) and current professional standards.

# ❤️ Patient Safety

Patient safety is the highest priority in every dispensing activity.

To minimise the risk of errors I consistently:

- Follow dispensing SOPs
- Carefully match medicines to prescriptions
- Verify medicine selection before assembly
- Check expiry dates
- Organise medicines clearly for pharmacist accuracy checking
- Maintain accurate documentation
- Communicate clearly with pharmacists whenever clarification is required

This approach supports safe medicine supply and protects patient wellbeing.
